How to Find Factors, GCF, and LCM

Factors go INTO a number. Multiples come FROM a number.

1.Find the factors

Factors are numbers that multiply together to make a number.

Example: factors of 12

1 × 12 = 122 × 6 = 123 × 4 = 12

Write every number you used:

1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

2.GCF

GCF = Greatest Common Factor

List the factors of BOTH numbers.

12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18

Circle the numbers in BOTH lists: 1, 2, 3, 6

The biggest one is 6.  GCF = 6

Use GCF to simplify fractions.

3.LCM

LCM = Least Common Multiple

Count by BOTH numbers.

4: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24… 6: 6, 12, 18, 24…

The first number in BOTH lists is 12.

LCM = 12

Use LCM to get matching bottoms for + or −.

Quick memory trick

FACTOR = goes INTO the number

MULTIPLE = count BY the number

GCF = biggest shared factor  |  LCM = first shared multiple
