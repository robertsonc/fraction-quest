Fractions: What Do I Do?

Step 1: Look at the math sign.

Add or subtract

Make the bottom numbers the same.

Then add or subtract the top numbers.

Example
38+14 Need 8 on the bottom. 4 × 2 = 8
Change the second fraction: 1 × 24 × 2=28
Now: 38+28=58

Multiply

Multiply straight across.

  • Top × top
  • Bottom × bottom

Whole number? Put it over 1.

58×41=208

Divide

Keep – Change – Flip

  • Keep the first fraction.
  • Change ÷ to ×.
  • Flip the second fraction.
58÷23 → 58×32

When you are done

Ask: Can I simplify?

  • Can the top and bottom both divide by the same number?
  • If yes, divide both by that number. Then ask again.
  • If no, you are done.

Easy memory rules

+ or − same bottoms first × straight across ÷ keep, change, flip
