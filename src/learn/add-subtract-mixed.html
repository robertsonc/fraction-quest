Adding and subtracting mixed numbers

Wholes first. Then the parts. Fill a bar or break a bar when you need to.

Pick a problem
Or your own

In words

A mixed number is wholes plus a part. Make the bottoms of the parts the same, then add or take away the wholes and the parts separately.

Adding: if the parts make more than one bar, fill a bar and count one more whole. Taking away: if the part is too small, break one whole bar into pieces first.
