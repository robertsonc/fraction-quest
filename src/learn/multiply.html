Multiplying: a part of a part

× means "of". Shade one way, then the other.

Pick a problem
Or your own
×

In words

Shade the first fraction in strips. Shade the second fraction in rows across them. The part shaded both ways is the answer.

That is why the rule is top × top and bottom × bottom. Then ask: can I simplify?
