Dividing: how many fit?

÷ asks how many of one thing fit into another.

Pick a problem
Or your own
÷

In words

Make the bottoms the same so the pieces match. Then count how many groups the size of the second fraction fit into the first. Pieces left over are a fraction of a group.

The shortcut, keep – change – flip, gives the same answer every time.
